Why qscommons builds quantum tools

Good quantum software is often tied to one vendor's hardware, hard to install, or written for people who already know the field.

qscommons builds the other kind: tools that install in one line, run on the computer you have, and teach the physics in their documentation. qsengine has shipped five releases this way, from eleven gates in 1.0.0 to mid-circuit measurement in 1.1.0.

Version 1.1.0
QuantumCircuit(num_qubits, num_clbits) gives a circuit classical bits.
measure(qubit, clbit) measures a qubit at any point, and reset(qubit) returns it to |0⟩.
Gates inside with qc.if_test(clbits, value): run only when the classical bits match.
expectation("ZZI") returns the expectation value of a Pauli string.
Recording a gate is about 2.3 times faster than in 1.0.2.
