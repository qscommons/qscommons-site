Entanglement
Two qubits are entangled when their joint state cannot be split into a state for each one. Measuring one then tells you about the other.
A Hadamard on qubit 0 and a CNOT from 0 to 1 give (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2, a Bell state. In qsengine it is two lines.
Each qubit on its own looks like a fair coin. Only the pair is special, because the two results always agree.
Running it 1000 times gives 513 of 00 and 487 of 11, and never 01 or 10.
Real output
{'00': 513, '11': 487} with seed=1.
Run with qsengine 1.1
